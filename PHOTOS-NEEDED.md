## How to give me the photos

**Easiest way** — put the pictures in a folder (anywhere, e.g. `Desktop\new-photos`)
and name each file with the **ID number** from the list:

```
497233.jpg      → Mug Cake Mix
498784.jpg      → Espresso
498760.jpg      → Mojito Signature
```

Then tell me the folder and I will crop, compress and install them all at once.

**If naming files is awkward**, just name them in plain words instead —
`espresso.jpg`, `mojito strawberry.png` — and I will match them up. Or hand me a
few at a time and say what each one is.

**You can also do it yourself, one at a time, inside the POS:**
Products → find the item → **Edit** → **Choose photo…** → pick the file → **Save
product**. It crops to a square and shrinks it automatically.

### What makes a good photo
- **Square** is ideal (I crop to square from the centre, so keep the drink centred).
- **At least 400 × 400 pixels.** Bigger is fine — I shrink it.
- **JPG or PNG.**
- **A plain white or very plain background** looks best in the grid and matches the
  26 photos already in the system.
- **No logos, no brand names, no other café's cups** — that is the whole reason
  these were removed.

### Want to go faster?
You do not need 63 separate photos. Tell me to **use one photo for a whole group**
and I will apply it to every item in that sub-category. For example a single good
mojito photo can cover all 9 mojitos, and one hot-coffee cup can cover all 12 hot
coffees. You would then only need about **14 photos** instead of 63.
